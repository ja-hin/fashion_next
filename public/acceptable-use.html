This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs how you may use Faishon.studio and the content you may generate on our platform. It applies to all users — individuals, brands, and partners — and supplements our Terms of Use. By using Faishon.studio, you agree to comply with this Policy.

1Identity and Likeness Protection

We take the protection of personal identity and likeness seriously. To prevent misuse of generative AI:

  • All models featured on Faishon.studio are 100% AI-generated synthetic individuals. None are based on real, identifiable people.
  • You may not use the platform to recreate, replicate, or approximate the likeness of any real, named, or identifiable individual — including public figures, celebrities, or private persons — in any generated output.
  • You may upload photographs containing real people (for example, on-model garment photographs) solely as garment, styling, or background references. Faces and likenesses in uploaded reference images are not reproduced in outputs; all generated models are synthetic.
  • Where any Uploaded Content contains the image or likeness of a real person, you represent and warrant that you have obtained that person's valid, verifiable consent (including a model release or equivalent permission where applicable) for the upload and processing of their image on this platform. You are solely responsible for obtaining and maintaining such consents, and any claim arising from the absence of such consent is covered by the indemnification obligations in Section 3.
  • The creation of deepfakes, non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII), or any content intended to impersonate or deceive is strictly prohibited.

2Content Safety

To ensure the integrity of generated outputs:

  • Every image or video generated through Faishon.studio is automatically screened by our NSFW classifier. Outputs flagged as containing nudity or sexually explicit material are blocked from delivery.
  • The platform is designed and intended for fashion ecommerce imagery — apparel and intimate apparel (including lingerie and swimwear) consistent with mainstream catalog standards comparable to imagery published by leading fashion retailers.

5Prohibited Use

You may not use Faishon.studio to create, request, or distribute:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We have a zero-tolerance policy and report violations to applicable authorities.

  • Sexual or explicit content beyond catalog-standard fashion imagery.
  • Content that infringes any third party's intellectual property or other rights.

Frequently asked questions

Answers, in plain language

Quick answers to the questions we hear most about this policy. The full policy text above always governs.

Are the models in Faishon.studio images real people?+

No. Every model featured on Faishon.studio is a 100% AI-generated synthetic individual. None are based on real, identifiable people — which means no model releases, no usage renewals, and no likeness disputes for the models we provide.

Can I upload photos of my garments being worn by a real model?+

Yes. On-model garment photographs are an accepted input — they are used solely as garment, styling, or background references. The real person's face and likeness are never reproduced in your outputs; the generated images always feature synthetic AI models. You must have the consent of any real person appearing in photos you upload.

Do I need a model release for people in my uploaded photos?+

Yes. If any content you upload contains a real person's image or likeness, you must have that person's valid, verifiable consent — a model release or equivalent permission. You are solely responsible for obtaining and keeping these consents, and claims arising from missing consent fall under your indemnification obligations.

Can I recreate a specific person, celebrity, or influencer as a model?+

No. Using the platform to recreate, replicate, or approximate the likeness of any real, named, or identifiable individual — public figure, celebrity, or private person — is strictly prohibited. This is a zero-tolerance rule: deepfake creation results in immediate account termination and may be reported to authorities.

Is lingerie and swimwear photography allowed?+

Yes. The platform supports apparel and intimate apparel — including lingerie and swimwear — consistent with mainstream catalog standards, comparable to imagery published by leading fashion retailers. Sexual or explicit content beyond catalog-standard fashion imagery is blocked.

How is inappropriate content prevented?+

Every image and video generated through Faishon.studio is automatically screened by our NSFW classifier before delivery. Outputs flagged as containing nudity or sexually explicit material are blocked. Generated content is also logged and may be reviewed in abuse investigations.

Can I upload a competitor's catalog photos to generate my own images?+

No — you may only upload content you own or have the rights to use. Uploading another brand's photography, designs, or trademarks without permission violates this policy, and you indemnify Faishon.studio against any resulting infringement claims. Rights holders can submit takedown notices to support@faishon.studio.

Who owns the images and videos I generate?+

You receive full rights to use your generated outputs commercially — listings, ads, social, marketing — provided you had the rights to the content you uploaded. If your upload infringed someone else's rights, no rights in the corresponding outputs are granted and you remain liable for resulting claims.

What is your policy on CSAM?+

Zero tolerance. Any attempt to create, request, or distribute child sexual abuse material results in immediate termination and is reported to applicable authorities. Reports of CSAM or non-consensual intimate imagery sent to us are prioritized for immediate review.

How do I report content that violates this policy or my rights?+

Email support@faishon.studio. We aim to respond within 48 hours. Rights holders should include: identification of the infringed work, the URL or identifier of the infringing content, and contact details with a good-faith statement of infringement.

What happens if I violate the Acceptable Use Policy?+

Violations can lead to account suspension or termination without refund of remaining credits. Severe violations — CSAM, non-consensual intimate imagery, or deepfake creation — result in immediate termination and may be reported to relevant authorities.

Can I remove the watermark from free trial images myself?+

No. Removing, cropping, or obscuring the watermark on trial outputs is prohibited under our Terms of Use. Buying any credit pack removes the watermark automatically — including from content you already generated during the trial.
