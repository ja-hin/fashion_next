What is Faishon.studio?+

Faishon.studio is an AI-powered fashion photography platform for Indian D2C brands and marketplace sellers. You upload garment photos — flat-lay, packshot, or on-model — and the platform generates photorealistic on-model catalog images and videos featuring AI-generated synthetic models, ready for Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, Ajio, Nykaa, and your own store.

Is Faishon.studio a subscription service?+

No. Faishon.studio runs on a prepaid, pay-per-use credit system — you buy a credit pack once and credits are deducted only when you generate content. There are no monthly fees, no auto-renewals, and no lock-in. Subscription plans may be introduced in the future, but prepaid packs are the current model.

How long are my credits valid?+

Purchased credits are valid for 12 months. Every new credit purchase extends the validity of your entire remaining balance to 12 months from that purchase date — so as long as you top up at least once a year, your credits never expire.

What are the free trial credits and why are my downloads watermarked?+

New accounts receive complimentary trial credits (currently 25) with access to all features. Images and videos generated on trial credits carry a visible watermark on download and are for evaluation only. The moment you buy any prepaid pack, the watermark is automatically removed — from your previously generated content as well as everything you generate afterwards.

Can I get a refund on unused credits?+

No. The free trial exists so you can fully evaluate output quality before paying, so credit pack purchases are final and non-refundable — whether credits are unused, partially used, or fully used, and including when you close your account. The exception: if you were charged in error (duplicate payment, payment debited without credits added), contact us within 7 days and we will refund the verified erroneous charge.

What happens if a generation fails?+

You are not charged for failed generations. If a generation fails due to a platform error or is blocked by automated AI safety systems, the credits are either not deducted or are automatically returned to your balance.

Payments are processed by Razorpay, supporting UPI, cards, netbanking, and wallets. We never receive, see, or store your card or payment instrument details — Razorpay handles them directly.

Yes. Can I transfer credits to another account or convert them to cash?+

No. Credits are a prepaid usage entitlement for Faishon.studio services only. They are non-transferable between accounts, cannot be resold, and have no cash value.

What happens to my account if I stop using it?+

Accounts inactive for 12 continuous months may be deactivated. We send at least 30 days' advance notice by email first — simply logging in before the deactivation date preserves your account and credit balance. Will credit pack prices change?+

Pack sizes, prices, and per-generation credit costs may be revised over time. Credits you have already purchased are always honoured at the balance you hold — price revisions only apply to future purchases and future per-generation costs.

Who can sign up for Faishon.studio?+

Faishon.studio is built for professional and commercial use by businesses, brands, and sellers, and users must be at least 18 years old. The platform is currently invite-only while in its early access phase. Each individual or business is entitled to one account with free trial credits.

Does the AI always produce perfect results?+

Generative AI has inherent limitations. Results vary with input quality and garment type, and complex draping — sarees, dhotis, unstitched fabrics — may not always render true-to-life. We do not guarantee that every generation matches your creative expectations, which is exactly why failed generations are never charged and the free trial exists.
