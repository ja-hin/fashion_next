At 3rd i Visuals Pvt. Ltd., we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through Faishon.studio, why we collect it, how we use it, and how we safeguard it. It is designed to comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and reflects internationally recognized data protection principles.

If you have questions, please contact us at studio.support@faishon.studio.

1Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to Faishon.studio, a platform operated by 3rd i Visuals Pvt. Ltd., a company based in Bangalore, India. Faishon.studio enables fashion brands, marketplace sellers, and agencies to generate AI-powered on-model fashion photography and videos from user-uploaded garment images.

2Definitions

  • Platform refers to Faishon.studio, including the web application, image and video generation, saved models, and all supporting services.
  • Uploaded Content means images and other material you upload, including garment photographs (flat-lay or on-model), sketches, and reference images.
  • Generated Outputs means AI-generated images and videos created from your Uploaded Content.
  • Saved Model means an AI-generated synthetic model saved to your account for reuse across shoots.
  • You or User refers to any business, team, or individual using our platform.

3Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide, bill, secure, and improve our services.

a. Information You Provide

  • Name and email address, via account signup or login;
  • Business details you choose to provide, including organization name and GST number (for GST-compliant invoicing);
  • Uploaded Content, used solely for generating your outputs;
  • Saved Model configurations and shoot settings;
  • Support requests, messages, and feedback you submit voluntarily.

Uploaded images containing people. Some Uploaded Content (such as on-model garment photographs) may contain images of real people. We process such images only as garment, styling, or background references to generate outputs featuring synthetic AI models — the faces and likenesses of real people in your uploads are not reproduced in Generated Outputs. As set out in our Acceptable Use Policy, you are responsible for ensuring you have the consent of any person appearing in content you upload.

b. Payment Information

Payments are processed by our third-party payment partner (currently Razorpay). Your card, UPI, and payment instrument details are collected and processed directly by the payment partner — we do not receive, store, or have access to them. We receive transaction confirmations, amounts, and invoicing details necessary to credit your account and issue invoices.

c. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and browser type;
  • Session tokens and authentication cookies;
  • Usage activity, such as features used, credits consumed, and generation history;
  • General location (region-level) based on IP address;
  • Technical logs, including generation logs used for billing accuracy, error diagnosis, and abuse prevention.

4How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Set up and manage your account and sessions;
  • Generate and deliver AI-powered images and videos from your uploads;
  • Track credit usage and billing, and issue GST-compliant invoices;
  • Maintain platform safety, including automated content screening and abuse investigation as described in our Acceptable Use Policy;
  • Respond to your support queries;
  • Improve platform features, reliability, and performance;
  • Send service communications, and product updates or marketing emails where permitted (you can opt out of marketing at any time).

We do not use your Uploaded Content, Generated Outputs, or Saved Models to train AI models. Your content is processed only to deliver the services you request.

5Third-Party AI Processing

Generating your outputs involves processing your Uploaded Content through third-party AI infrastructure providers. Your uploads and generation instructions are transmitted to these providers solely to produce your requested outputs. Under the commercial API terms we operate on, these providers do not use your content to train their models. We select providers with appropriate data protection commitments and share only what is necessary to fulfil your generation requests. A list of our current infrastructure providers is available to customers on request, subject to confidentiality.

6Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain secure login sessions, remember your preferences, and understand how the platform is used so we can improve it. Analytics data is used in aggregate to improve onboarding, features, and performance — we do not sell your personal data or share your uploads with analytics providers. Most browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through their settings; disabling essential cookies may affect platform functionality.

7Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure with encryption in transit and appropriate access controls. Access to user data is limited to authorized personnel and only for resolving support requests, operating the platform, or investigating abuse.

In the event of a personal data breach affecting you, we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities as required by the DPDP Act and other applicable law.

8Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your uploads, shoots, Generated Outputs, and Saved Models remain stored in your workspace until you delete them or your account is closed or deactivated.
  • You can delete individual items from your dashboard at any time.
  • You may request deletion of your entire account, which results in permanent deletion of associated personal data, except where we are legally required to retain it (for example, tax and invoicing records) or where retention is necessary for abuse investigation or legal compliance, as described in our Acceptable Use Policy.
  • Accounts inactive for 12 months may be deactivated as described in our Terms of Use; stored content may be permanently deleted following deactivation.
  • Technical and generation logs are retained for limited periods for billing accuracy, security, and abuse prevention.

9Your Rights

In accordance with the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed;
  • Correction: Request updates or corrections to inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements;
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for processing based on consent, including opting out of marketing emails;
  • Grievance Redressal: Raise a complaint about how your data is handled (see Section 10);
  • Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity, as provided under the DPDP Act.

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at studio.support@faishon.studio. We aim to respond to most requests within 72 hours.

10Grievance Redressal

In accordance with applicable Indian law, including the DPDP Act and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, you may contact our Grievance Officer for any complaint regarding your personal data or content on the platform:

Grievance Officer: [Name] Email: support@faishon.studio Address: 3rd i Visuals Pvt. Ltd., Bangalore, India

We will acknowledge grievances within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days, or within shorter timelines where required by law (complaints concerning non-consensual intimate imagery are actioned on a priority basis as described in our Acceptable Use Policy).

11Children's Privacy

Faishon.studio is built strictly for professional and commercial use. We do not target or knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 18. If we discover that such data has been submitted, we will take immediate steps to delete it.

12International Users

Faishon.studio primarily serves users in India, and your data may be processed in India and in the jurisdictions where our cloud and AI infrastructure providers operate. Regardless of where you access the platform from, your data is handled in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

13Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or product features. When we make material changes, we will notify you via email or in-app notice, and the updated policy will be published with a new "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the platform after the update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Frequently asked questions

Answers, in plain language

Quick answers to the questions we hear most about this policy. The full policy text above always governs.

Do you use my uploaded images to train AI models?+

No. We do not use your uploaded images, generated outputs, or saved models to train AI models — yours or anyone else's. Your content is processed only to deliver the generations you request. The third-party AI infrastructure we use operates under commercial API terms that do not permit training on customer content either.

Who can see my uploaded garment photos and generated images?+

Only you, through your account workspace. Your content is not visible to other users, is not published anywhere by us, and internal access is limited to authorized personnel for support, platform operation, or abuse investigation.

What happens to photos I upload that contain real people?+

On-model garment photographs are processed only as garment, styling, or background references. The faces and likenesses of real people in your uploads are never reproduced in generated outputs — all generated models are synthetic AI individuals. You are responsible for having the consent of any person appearing in photos you upload, as set out in our Acceptable Use Policy.

Where is my data stored?+

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure with encryption in transit and access controls. Because generation uses third-party AI infrastructure, some processing may occur in the jurisdictions where those providers operate. A list of our current infrastructure providers is available to customers on request, subject to confidentiality.

Can I delete my uploads and generated images?+

Yes, at any time, from your dashboard — individual items or your entire account. Account deletion permanently removes associated personal data, except records we are legally required to retain (such as tax and invoicing records) or data needed for abuse investigation.

Do you see or store my card details?+

No. Payments are handled entirely by our payment partner (currently Razorpay). We receive only transaction confirmations, amounts, and invoicing details needed to credit your account and issue GST invoices.

What cookies and analytics do you use?+

We use cookies for secure login sessions, preferences, and aggregate analytics that help us improve the platform. We do not sell personal data and do not share your uploads with analytics providers. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Is Faishon.studio compliant with India's DPDP Act?+

Yes — this Privacy Policy is designed around the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including your rights to access, correction, erasure, consent withdrawal, grievance redressal, and nomination. We also follow internationally recognized data protection principles for users outside India.

How do I raise a privacy complaint?+

Write to our Grievance Officer at support@faishon.studio. We acknowledge grievances within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days, with priority handling for complaints involving non-consensual imagery, in line with Indian law.

Will you notify me if there is a data breach?+

Yes. In the event of a personal data breach affecting you, we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities as required by the DPDP Act and other applicable law.

Do you send marketing emails?+

We send essential service communications (billing, security, major changes) to all users. Product updates and marketing emails are sent only where permitted, and you can opt out of marketing at any time — opting out never affects service emails.

How long do you keep my data after I stop using the platform?+

Your workspace content stays until you delete it or your account is closed. Accounts inactive for 12 months may be deactivated (with 30 days' email notice), after which stored content may be permanently deleted. Technical and generation logs are kept for limited periods for billing accuracy, security, and abuse prevention.
